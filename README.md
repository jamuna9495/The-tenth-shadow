# The Tenth Shadow

For nearly **35 years**, an old women’s hostel had remained locked and abandoned. There was a strange story connected to that place.

During the daytime, the building looked like an ordinary old structure. But after **11:11 p.m.**, strange sounds could be heard from inside the hostel. People who followed those sounds never returned.

Because of this, many people believed that the abandoned hostel was haunted.

One day, a girl went there to find out the truth. She was warned not to enter the place at night. However, she decided to investigate the mystery herself.

According to the old records, **nine women had died in that hostel 35 years ago**. The police records stated that their deaths were suicides.

But there was something strange about the case.

All nine women had been living in the same hostel, and something mysterious had happened to them before their deaths. The old records contained several missing details, as though someone had deliberately hidden the truth.

The girl continued investigating the case.

She discovered that the hostel had once been occupied by several young women. Their photographs, belongings and personal records were still hidden inside the abandoned building.

Among the records, she found something particularly disturbing.

The documents mentioned **ten women**, but the official records only listed **nine deaths**.

She wondered:

**“If nine women died here… who was the tenth woman?”**

The mystery became even more frightening when she entered the hostel.

She suddenly saw the figures of several women standing inside the dark corridor.

She counted them.

**One… two… three…**

There were **ten women**.

But according to the official records, only nine women had ever lived there.

The girl was terrified.

One of the women slowly looked at her and said:

**“We have been waiting for you.”**

She could not understand what they meant.

Then another woman whispered:

**“You are the reason we are still here.”**

The girl looked around in fear.

She suddenly realized that the ten women were not trying to hurt her.

They were waiting for her to remember something.

She slowly began to remember the past.

Twenty-seven-year-old **Yazhini** had been investigating the abandoned hostel for years. But she had never realized that the mystery was connected to her own past.

As she continued looking through the old records, she discovered that the nine women had died **35 years ago**.

The police had officially recorded their deaths as suicide.

But the evidence inside the hostel suggested otherwise.

Someone had murdered them.

And someone had deliberately erased the truth from the records.

Yazhini then discovered something even more shocking.

She was not alone inside her own mind.

She had **ten different personalities** living within her.

Each personality had a different nature, memory and emotion.

Yazhini slowly understood that those personalities were somehow connected to the nine women who had died in the hostel.

Then she heard a voice behind her.

She turned around.

There was no one there.

But the voice whispered:

**“You remember now.”**

Yazhini looked into the mirror.

For a moment, she saw herself.

Then, behind her reflection, she saw **ten women** standing silently.

She counted them again.

Nine of them were the women who had died in the hostel.

But the tenth woman was different.

It was **Yazhini herself**.

Then a final voice whispered:

**“You were never investigating our story, Yazhini.”**

A pause.

**“You were remembering your own story.”*
